# Community Health Worker Assistance Platform

## About the Project

The Community Health Worker Assistance Platform is a proposed software solution designed to support Community Health Workers (CHWs) in managing patient information, accessing health-related resources, tracking activities, receiving notifications, and generating reports.

## Objectives

- Provide a centralized platform for Community Health Workers.
- Simplify patient information management.
- Provide easy access to health-related information.
- Track patient visits and CHW activities.
- Provide reminders and notifications.
- Generate reports for monitoring and management.

## Proposed Modules

1. User Authentication
2. CHW Dashboard
3. Patient Management
4. Health Assistance
5. Activity Tracking
6. Notifications and Reminders
7. Reports
