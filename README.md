# # Community Health Worker Assistance Platform

## Project Overview

The Community Health Worker Assistance Platform is a proposed software system designed to support Community Health Workers (CHWs) in managing patient information, accessing health-related resources, tracking activities, receiving notifications, and generating reports.

## Objectives

- Provide a centralized platform for Community Health Workers.
- Simplify patient information management.
- Provide easy access to health-related information.
- Track patient visits and CHW activities.
- Provide reminders and notifications.
- Generate reports for monitoring and management.

## Proposed Modules

- User Authentication
- CHW Dashboard
- Patient Management
- Health Assistance
- Activity Tracking
- Notifications and Reminders
- Reports
